1985

By 1985, Nelson Mandela had spent more than 20 years in prison during the struggle against apartheid, South Africa’s system of racial segregation.

Then the president offered to release him.

But the offer came with a condition: he had to reject armed struggle while apartheid still stood.

Mandela said no. His daughter Zindzi read his answer at a rally in Soweto. He said:

He remained in prison for another five years. In 1990, he was released unconditionally.

1993

Nelson Mandela was awarded the Nobel Peace Prize, who working alongside Frederik Willem de Klerk helped safely end apartheid and build a new democracy in South Africa
