Marie and Pierre Curie had evidence for a new radioactive element. But they needed enough to study it.

It was very rare: Marie Curie had to process tonnes of residue from a uranium-rich ore called pitchblende.

She separated the material, kept the most radioactive part, then started again. And again. And again.
